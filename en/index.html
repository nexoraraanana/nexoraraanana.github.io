What we install

Network & Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi in every room with wired access points, Cat6 cabling through existing conduits when possible, and a tidy network cabinet.

  • Access points (UniFi, TP-Link Omada)
  • Cat6 cabling & wall sockets
  • PoE switches, routers, cabinets

Security cameras

IP cameras recorded at your place, watched from your phone, set up the secure way.

  • Up to 4K, depending on the model
  • Person & vehicle detection on compatible models
  • No monthly fee for local recording

Smart home

Lights, shutters, AC and the water heater on smart schedules, Shabbat mode included, running locally.

  • Smart relays & shutter modules (Shelly, Sonoff)
  • Leak, door and motion sensors (Aqara)
  • Home Assistant, no cloud needed

Intercom & access

See who's at the gate, talk from your phone and open remotely.

  • Video intercoms (Hikvision)
  • Remote gate opening*
  • Keypads, badge readers, smart locks
* with a compatible electric gate or lock

Questions

Where do you work?

Ra'anana, Kfar Saba, Hod Hasharon, Herzliya and the rest of the Sharon.

How much is the Home Check?

It's free for the first 5 homes in Ra'anana. After the visit you get a clear quote, with no obligation.

Do I need a monthly subscription for cameras?

No. Recording happens at your place, on the recorder. The manufacturers' apps to watch remotely are free; cloud storage is optional.

Can you use my existing cables or conduits?

Often, yes: we check where the existing conduits go and pull Cat6 through them when they allow it.

What about electrical work?

Anything on the 230 V network is done by a licensed electrician.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes: shops, offices, clinics and rentals, as well as homes.
